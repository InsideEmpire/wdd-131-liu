Overview

Purpose

The purpose of the SolarTint website is to explain the app and show how it works. It helps users understand the idea of adjusting screen color based on the sun and why it is better than Night Shift.

Audience

The audience includes hackathon judges, macOS users, and people who spend a lot of time on screens and want to reduce eye strain.

Dynamic elements

The website uses JavaScript to create interactive features such as signle page appliction and display my newest updates on GitHub.
